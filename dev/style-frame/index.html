The siege screen, drawn for real

One screen from The Siege, volley 2, at landscape phone size. Everything is drawn in code except the three plushies, which are illustrated sprites in the key-art style. If the two sit together well, the hybrid works.

Drawn in code

  • The room, the rug and the launcher.
  • All four piece families: books, toy blocks, dominoes, cards.
  • The crayon layer: lava that boils at 12 fps, flames, the wall drawings, aim dots, the impact burst.
  • The interface: sticker button, team bandanas, tape label, crayon clock, matchbox of ammo.

Illustrated sprites

  • Brumm, Hoppel and Socke, generated with the app icon as the style reference and cut out of a magenta background.
  • They wear the green team's striped bandanas, because this is the opponent's fort.
  • They breathe a little. Only plushies squash; real pieces never do.

What to look at

  • Do the plushies belong on the code-drawn pieces, or do they look pasted on?
  • At phone size: can you tell cards, dominoes, blocks and books apart?
  • Is the lava loud enough to read as danger, and the wall drawings quiet enough?
  • The lava has risen one cell up the fort after volley 1. Does that read?
